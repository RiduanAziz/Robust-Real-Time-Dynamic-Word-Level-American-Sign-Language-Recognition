    Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Findings — SignFlow WLASL-100¶

    Document Version: 2.0.0 (Empirical Analysis Report)
    Author: Lead Research & ML Systems Engineer
    Date: October 2026
    Artifact Dependencies: - results/eda/class_distribution.csv - results/eda/sequence_statistics.csv - results/eda/landmark_quality.csv - results/eda/feature_statistics.csv - results/eda/plots/

    1. Executive Summary¶

    A comprehensive exploratory data analysis was conducted directly across the precomputed MediaPipe landmark tensors and manifest metadata for the 2,038 samples comprising WLASL-100.

    Key Findings:¶

    1. Modality Asymmetry:
    2. Body Pose: 99.4% average frame detection rate.
    3. Face Mesh: 97.3% average frame detection rate.
    4. Right Hand: 63.6% average frame detection rate.
    5. Left Hand: 32.5% average frame detection rate. Implication: ASL exhibits high right-hand dominance. Forcing uniform concatenation without validity gating introduces severe zero-fill noise for left-hand non-dominant signs.
    6. Temporal Dynamics:
    7. Video clip lengths range from 9 frames (0.36 s) to 178 frames (7.12 s).
    8. The median sequence length is 52 frames (~2.08 seconds).
    9. Standard resampling to \(T=64\) frames preserves temporal resolution across >85% of samples without downsampling information loss.
    10. Class Balance:
    11. Training sample count per class ranges from 9 to 25 samples.
    12. Validation and test sets maintain near-uniform per-class support (2 to 4 samples per class).

    From results/eda/sequence_statistics.csv:

    Architectural Implication:¶

    Configuring the models to sequence_length = 64 with linear temporal interpolation and causal padding accommodates the central distribution of ASL isolated signs while maintaining real-time latency (<40 ms).

    4. Class Distribution & Imbalance¶

    From results/eda/class_distribution.csv: - Top-frequency classes: book, drink, computer, before, chair (22–25 training samples). - Lower-frequency classes: thursday, africa, gold, glasses (9–12 training samples). - Imbalance Ratio: Maximum/Minimum ratio is \(\sim 2.78:1\). - Mitigation Strategy: - Training uses label smoothing (\(\epsilon = 0.08\)) and Cosine Annealing scheduler. - Test set is locked and strictly balanced (257 samples across 100 classes).
